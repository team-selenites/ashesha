    What the shapes mean

    • Clear detectionThe signal is clear in this image.
    • FaintA signal is present but weak.
    • Not detectedThe image exists but shows no signal.
    • No observationNo image covers this place and time.
    • FlaggedThe image exists, but its measurement is flagged as failed, for example because some pixels are masked.

    SPHEREx photographs each part of the sky in a different colour. A change in brightness between two pictures is not always a real change in the object.

    SPHEREx sees infrared light that our eyes cannot. Each picture is taken in one colour, measured in microns (millionths of a metre). Each mark is one picture; its shape is its status.
