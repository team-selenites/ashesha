Something in the sky is moving. Can you spot it?

Every six months, NASA's SPHEREx telescope photographs the entire sky in 102 colours of light your eyes can't see. Play its pictures in date order and the still sky starts to move: comets drift, nearby stars creep, and some brighten and fade.
